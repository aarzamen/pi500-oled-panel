Case model and printing notes

The supplied STL has two closed, watertight parts: a 56 × 38 × 9.343 mm outer bezel and a 51.104 × 33.163 × 6.041 mm front shell. The exact source meshes are available in Original STL view. That reference never changes.

The editable design is a parametric reconstruction. Its window, flexing button tabs and mounting layout follow the source; corner curves and fastening details are newly generated. The starting shell is 8 mm deep to leave room for the approximate board stack and cover lip. It is not an exact surface edit of the original STL.

Easy links wall thicknesses and radii in their starting proportions. Intermediate exposes individual part sizes. Expert adds offsets, fit clearances and catch details. Changing level never changes geometry. Each ↺ resets a field to the chosen starting preset; Reset all also restores its outer dimensions. A linked reset leaves any enlarged outer dimensions in place until Reset all.

The ZIP face style uses the supplied 096+4btn - bat model's 45° beveled screen opening and rounded button apertures, plus a separate connected button strip. The throat shapes and cap proportions follow that source. Spacing and location are adapted to the existing PCB mounts; this is not a copy of the ZIP's entire enclosure. ZIP reference includes all five exact source meshes for individual comparison. The button strip exports as a fourth STL when selected.

PCB mounting controls set the outside diameter of all four standoffs and the diameter of their blind screw holes. The hole value is the printed pilot diameter, not a nominal screw size. Larger posts must clear the button flexures and screen.

The backplate has a movable, resizable opening for a row of straight DuPont connectors projecting perpendicular to the PCB. The upper case wall is continuous. The eight-way connector guide uses an approximate 2.54 mm pitch, fixed at Y = 10.5 mm; moving the aperture does not move the guide or the real pins.

The snap-fit option uses flexible side arms and mating catches, inspired by the supplied Raspberry Pi camera enclosure. Slip-fit uses a locating lip with positive clearance; it does not latch. Screw-fastened adds matching external ears; check fastener dimensions and length on a test print.

Clearance is radial: 0.25 mm means 0.25 mm per side. A CAD fit does not establish a printed fit. Check the board, solder joints, switch travel, wire connector, print orientation and material flexibility. Existing source electronics are only a guide; resizing the housing does not resize the real PCB.

Exported STL units are millimeters. The cover and bezel are flipped face-down and each part is moved to the print bed for export; the camera, exploded view, color, and PCB guide never change the exported solid. Prototype support/bridging and snap flexibility in your slicer before printing.
