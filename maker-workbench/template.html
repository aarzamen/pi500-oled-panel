Keep these datums straight

4.75 mm is the PCB right edge to the nearest fixed housing edge. The 14.25 mm button center uses an estimated outline and housing width.

Approx. 40 × 24 mm mount pitch still needs a precise remeasure.

2.65 / 1.8 mm are the moving button peak / fixed housing heights above the front of the PCB.

Eight pins: power, I²C, and four independent keys. Four wires do not carry this complete panel.

Open the correct rear panel for straight DuPont plugs. An empty-case latch test does not establish assembled closure.
